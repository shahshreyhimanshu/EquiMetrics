EquiMetrics is a next-generation stock market intelligence platform built specifically for Indian retail investors and traders who want to make data-backed, independent financial decisions.
Instead of issuing speculative buy or sell tips, EquiMetrics serves as a high-speed analytical toolkit that combines real-time exchange data (NSE/BSE) with AI-powered data processing. Whether you are tracking promoter insider buying, analyzing options chains, or reading quarterly earnings, EquiMetrics organizes the market’s clutter into structured, visually intuitive dashboards.
Key Modules Inside EquiMetrics
1. AI Earnings & Corporate Filing Summarizer
Instantly process lengthy quarterly earnings transcripts. Get factual bullet points detailing YoY revenue growth, profit margins, debt changes, and management commentary in seconds—free from analyst bias or artificial price targets.
2. SEBI Insider & Executive Trade Tracker
Stay informed when company promoters, directors, or key executives buy, sell, or pledge their own shares. EquiMetrics processes public SEBI SAST and PIT disclosures into an instant alert feed.
3. Options Chain & Volatility Heatmaps
Track market liquidity and sentiment using visual heatmaps for Call/Put Open Interest (OI), Implied Volatility (IV), and exchange-disclosed Bulk/Block deal data.
4. Natural Language Rule Screener
Filter stocks across the NSE/BSE using custom metric rules (e.g., "Find companies with P/E under 20, ROCE over 15%, and zero promoter pledge").
5. Visual Strategy Backtester & Demo Portfolio
Build, simulate, and test custom entry/exit rules against historical market data without writing code, and practice strategies risk-free inside a virtual ₹10,00,000 paper-trading environment.
6. Multi-Broker Portfolio Aggregator
Connect holdings across brokers (Zerodha, Groww, Angel One) using India's secure Account Aggregator ecosystem to view total net worth and sector exposure in a single dashboard.
